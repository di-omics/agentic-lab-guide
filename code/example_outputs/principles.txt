regular, 100 uL
  aspirate at top speed, 1 mm above the bottom
  dispense at top speed, 1 mm above the bottom
  blowout  20 uL
  - Start with regular parameters, maximum speed for everything.
  - Sufficient blowout volume, to ensure proper liquid handling.
  - Aspirate low enough that the liquid is not missed.
  - Dispense at a Z height where every droplet catches the well.

cells, 100 uL
  aspirate at top speed, 1 mm above the bottom
  dispense at 60 percent of top speed, 3 mm above the bottom
  blowout  20 uL
  - Start with regular parameters, maximum speed for everything.
  - Sufficient blowout volume, to ensure proper liquid handling.
  - Aspirate low enough that the liquid is not missed.
  - Gentler into cells: 50 to 70 percent of maximum speed.
  - Far enough that the tips do not scrape the cells.
  - Close enough that the droplet dips in the well.

lysis, 100 uL
  aspirate at top speed, 1 mm above the bottom
  dispense at top speed, 1 mm above the bottom
  blowout  20 uL
  - Start with regular parameters, maximum speed for everything.
  - Sufficient blowout volume, to ensure proper liquid handling.
  - Aspirate low enough that the liquid is not missed.
  - A forceful dispense for lysis.
  - Dispense at a Z height where every droplet catches the well.

TOP_SPEED is not set. It differs with the instrument, the tips
and the liquid. Set yours in principles.py.
